See the Q-learning update +
Q(s, a) ← Q(s, a) + α [r + γ max Q(s′, a′) − Q(s, a)]

α controls learning speed. γ values future rewards. The maximum uses legal moves; a terminal state has no future value. During play, the trained agent selects a legal move with the highest Q-value and breaks ties at random. Its saved policy stays fixed while you play.

The exact training setup and measured results are included in the project notebook and report.
